# First phone playtest

This is a debug preview. Keep an earlier installation and choose an in-place update if Android accepts the matching signer. Uninstalling removes local progress. No phone installation or update has been verified in this build environment.

## First ten minutes

1. Open **How to Fly → Start Guided Practice**. Can you complete steering, a green-zone cut and a release without outside explanation?
2. Challenge Mira, Dev and Riya in the first chapter. Can you explain why you won or lost? Does each rival feel different?
3. Replay one stage and try for three medals. Do clean cuts and tension management feel under your control?
4. Pause during a duel, switch apps, return, and resume. Check that no held control is stuck.
5. Try left-handed controls and reduced motion. Verify notch-safe buttons are visible and easy to reach.

## Progress and stability

- Check a win unlocks the next rooftop and losing a replay keeps your best medals.
- Win a City Cup round, return to home, close and reopen the app. Resume should start the next saved round with full string.
- Run several matches offline. Guest gameplay, campaign, settings and local saves should work without Google services.
- Watch for overheating, stuttering, unreadable text, quiet/missing cues and touch conflicts on your particular phone.
- Keep the APK version, phone model, Android version and steps for any issue. Do not share account credentials or tokens.

## Before deciding to monetize

Have several target players try the game without coaching. Record where they get confused, whether they voluntarily replay, which rivals feel unfair, and whether progression is worth returning to. Automated battle checks do not answer those questions. Test live provider integrations, privacy/audience decisions and release compliance separately before any production ad rollout.
